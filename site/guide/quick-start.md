# Quick Start

After installing and restarting, you're ready to go.

## Open the Library

Double-click the **Dad's Library** icon on the desktop.

A console window appears saying *"Getting your books ready..."*. After a moment, your browser opens to the home page.

::: tip First Launch
The very first launch builds the FlowState Reader image from source, which takes a few minutes. After that, subsequent launches are fast.
:::

## The Home Page

The home page shows two large cards:

- **Read & Listen Together** — opens Storyteller (ebooks synced with audiobook narration)
- **Focus Reader** — opens FlowState Reader (one-word-at-a-time reading)

Click either card to open that app. Use your browser's back button to return.

## Adding Books to Storyteller

1. Open **Read & Listen Together** from the home page.
2. Create an account (this is local — only on your PC).
3. Upload an `.epub` ebook file.
4. Optionally add the matching audiobook file for synced narration.

## Stopping the Library

Use the **Stop Dad's Library** entry in the Start Menu, or just shut down the PC — the containers will stop automatically and restart next time you click the shortcut.

## Accessing from Other Devices

The library is accessible from any device on the same network. Find your PC's IP address and browse to `http://<your-pc-ip>:8080`. The home page dynamically adjusts its links to use the correct hostname.
